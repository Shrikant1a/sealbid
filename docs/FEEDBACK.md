# Structured Feedback (Level 5 Requirement)

The Midnight Level 5 Full Moon milestone requires gathering structured, concrete feedback from users on the Preprod network. 

## Feedback Gathering Methodology
- Users were provided a Google Form link after completing a bid.
- The form asked specific questions about the wallet connection experience, understanding of the zero-knowledge privacy guarantees, and overall UX.
